# **Classes**

This section details each class. Your chosen class grants the following:

## **Class Traits**

Class traits are improvable traits like **Hit Points, Stamina,** and **Mana**.

### **Starting Hit Points**

Each class has its own starting Hit Points. Regardless of this value, it is a Tier 1 trait when you choose the class.

### **Starting Stamina**

Each class has its own starting Stamina. Like Hit Points, it is a Tier 1 trait when you choose the class.

### **Starting Mana**

If your class casts spells, it has a starting Mana. Like Hit Points, it is a Tier 1 trait when you choose the class.

## **Evasion**

The value for your Evasion. Unlike Traits, Evasion does not have a Tier and thus is not improved by using Skill Points.

## **Damage Thresholds**

The values for your **Minor, Moderate, and Major** damage thresholds. For all classes, the starting value for your **Minor** damage threshold is 0\.

## **Tier 1 Class Feature**

Each class has a unique class feature at Tier 1

## **Spell or Technique List**

Each class has a specific list of spells or techniques to choose from.

## **Starting Equipment**

Each class has a unique set of starting items.

# **Subclasses**

Each class has two subclasses. Your chosen subclass grants the following:

## **Tier 1 Subclass Feature**

Each subclass has a unique subclass feature at Tier 1

## **Primary Trait**

Each subclass has a trait it uses any time a spell or technique calls for a **check**, otherwise known as a **Primary check**.

## **Class Features**

### **Helping Hand**

## *Tier 1 Class Feature*

You are always nearby, willing to aid your allies. When an ally is in Very Close range and fails a check, you can spend 1 Stamina to add a d6 to their roll. Describe how you help them with their check.

### **Close Call**

## *Tier 2 Class Feature*

Just when your enemies think they have you, you slip through their aggression. Once per session, when an enemy succeeds on an Attack check against you, spend 1 Miracle to avoid the attack.

## **Inspiring**

A beacon of hope or triumph to those who see you, you excel at bringing out the best in others

**Primary Trait:** Charisma

### **Inspiration**

## *Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

Once per session, spend 3 Stamina and describe how you rally your party to face the odds with renewed fervor. All of your allies who witness you gain a **Miracle**.

### **Stay With Me**

## *Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Your presence gives your allies greater conviction to stay alive. When an ally within Close range would make a Death Action, spend 1 Miracle to let them regain 1 Hit Point.

## **Paragon**

An agile warrior feared by those who stand against your values, you strike faster than the eye can see.

**Primary Trait:** Agility

### **Repetition**

## *Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you make an attack, you can spend 3 Stamina to make a number of additional attacks equal to your Hero **Tier**.

### **Dash**

## *Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Your body is honed for agile movements. When you make an Agility check to move far distances, double your Agility modifier added to the check.

## **Class Features**

### **Grit**

## *Tier 1 Class Feature*

Part of your training is withstanding and dealing pain. At the start of a session, you have a number of **Grit** equal to your Primary Trait modifier. When you make a **check** using your Primary Trait, you may spend a **Grit** to add a d8 to the **check**.

### **Opportunity**

## *Tier 2 Class Feature*

You can react to enemies that attempt to escape your grasp. If an enemy within Melee range moves away from you, spend a **Miracle** to inflict one of the following effects.

* They are **Restrained** by you and can not move.  
* They are **Exposed** by you.  
* You deal damage to them with one of your equipped weapons.  
* You move along with them.

## **Tough**

You are trained to resist all threats, physical or magical, so that you may take the pain that others can not.

**Primary Trait:** Strength

### **Withstand**

## *Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

No pain is too great. When you take damage, spend a **Grit** to reduce it by one threshold.

### **Retaliate**

## *Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Strike back at your enemy. Your **Opportunity** feature may now be triggered when an enemy within **Melee** range deals damage to you.

## **Brutal**

You are trained to inflict unbelievable damage upon your targets, slaying them before they can do the same to you.

**Primary Trait:** Strength

### **Eviscerate**

## *Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

None can resist your offense. When you succeed an **Attack Action**, spend a **Grit** to roll an additional damage die.

### **Onslaught**

## *Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Strike while they are weak. Your **Opportunity** feature may now be triggered when you succeed an **Attack Action** against an enemy within **Melee** range.

# **Spells and Techniques**

This section details each spell and technique. Each spell and technique has the following.

## **Class Requirement**

Each spell and technique is available for a unique class.

## **Tier Requirement**

Each spell and technique has a **Tier Requirement** based on its **Class Requirement**. For instance, a **Priest** spell with a Tier 2 requirement can only be taken if you are a Tier 2 Priest.

## **Learn Cost**

Each spell and technique costs **Skill Points** in order to learn.

## **Use Cost**

Each spell and technique cost resources to use. Spells use **Mana** and techniques use **Stamina**.

## **Tier**

The Tier of the spell or technique denotes how much you can augment it when you use it.

## **Description**

The description for the effect of the spell or technique. Sections written in **Bold** denote **Augment Effects.**

# **Conditions**

## **Bloodied**

A Bloodied creature has less than or equal to half of its Hit Points remaining. 

## **Hidden**

A Hidden creature has its exact position obscured from its adversaries. While Hidden, you have advantage on Attack checks. The Hidden Condition automatically ends when:

* You or a creature you are hiding from moves into an area where there is clear line of sight.  
* You make an Attack, cast a spell, or use a Technique that targets a creature you are hiding from.

## **Exposed**

An Exposed creature has its defenses lowered. While Exposed, you can not reduce damage by spending Stamina. This does not prevent Actions, Spells, or Techniques that can reduce damage.

## **Dazed**

A Dazed creature is momentarily unsure of their surroundings. While Dazed, you can not take Actions. The Dazed Condition can be ended by spending 1 Stamina.

This Condition can not be removed via a check or as a GM move.

## **Disarmed**

A Disarmed creature has either lost their weapon or is unable to utilize it. While Disarmed, you can not make **attack checks**.

## **Drained**

A Drained creature finds it more difficult to utilize their abilities. While Drained, each point of Mana or Stamina you spend costs 1 additional point.

## **Restrained**

A Restrained creature is prevented from moving. While Restrained, you can not move.

## **Taunted**

A Taunted creature is focused on the taunting creature. While Taunted, you must take harmful Actions against the creature that Taunted you. If you are Taunted by multiple creatures, you can choose any of them to harm.

## **Poisoned**

A Poisoned creature has toxins in its body, slowly killing it. Poisoned can be stacked. While Poisoned, you lose 1 Stamina for each stack of Poison whenever you take an Action. When you take an Action, 1 stack of Poison is removed.

This Condition can not be removed via a check or as a GM move. Instead, spending 1 Miracle or 1 Doom as an Action removes 1d4 \+ your Total Tier stacks of Poison.

## **Aflame**

An Aflame creature is on fire. Aflame can be stacked. While Aflame, you take 1 damage for each stack of Aflame whenever you take an Action.

This Condition can not be removed via a check or as a GM move. Instead, spending 1 Stamina as an action removes 1d4 \+ your Total Tier stacks of Aflame.

**Bleeding**  
A Bleeding creature is losing large quantities of blood. While Bleeding, you lose 1 Hit Point whenever you take an Action.

This condition can be removed by regaining Hit Points. However, you regain 1 less Hit Point.

**Stanced**  
A Stanced creature has entered a position that grants them a certain effect, often determined by the Technique or feature that caused them to enter this stance.

If you would gain the Stanced condition while already Stanced, you lose the previous Stance’s effects.

# **Races**

## **Human**

Humans are short-lived but highly innovative and curious people. They are highly sociable, compensating for their short lifespans by passing on their knowledge to the next generation. As a result, their species has advanced rapidly in fields like medicine, engineering, and magic.

Human adults often reach heights between 4 and 7 feet tall, and live for up to a century.

**Generational Knowledge.** You gain an additional Experience when you create your character.

**Adaptive.** Once per session, you can deactivate one of your Spells or Techniques and activate a different ability.

## **Elf**

Elves are a rare, often isolated people who are capable of living for thousands of years. To them, months could feel like a mere day. Elves tend to have difficulty with boredom and feel little urgency outside of life-threatening events. Being living witnesses to history, they are often well learned and well travelled. 

Elven adults often grow to be between 4 and 5 feet tall, and live for so long that many could consider them ageless.

**Expertise.** One of your Experiences starts with a \+4 modifier instead of a \+2.

**Unphased.** When you make a Basic Trait **check** to avoid or remove a Condition, you may spend a Stamina to add 1d4 to the total.

## **Dwarf**

Dwarves are naturally strong and durable people, often possessing talents in physically demanding professions. Dwarven culture often pushes them to master a single skill like blacksmithing or combat. Though they live for centuries, many still seek companionship with humans and their own kind.

Dwarven adults often grow to be between 	3 and 4 feet tall and live for about 4 centuries.

**Durable.** You have a \+0/+1/+2 to your damage thresholds.

**Inventive.** When you invent your own Spell or Technique to learn, the learning cost of the ability is reduced by 1\.
