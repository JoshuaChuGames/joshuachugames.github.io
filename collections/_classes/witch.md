***Far from the bustle of civilization or hidden in the alcoves of the city, those who study the old magic call themselves Witches. Rather than learn through academic scholarship, Witches experiment with the means around them, commune with spirits to learn their ways, and force the world to bend to their will through the ancient way of Magick. Wielding hexes, curses, and their signature magick, these mages often find themselves persecuted by society or forced to hide their gifts to stay within it.***

*You must have an Intelligence score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.*

## Class Features

As a Witch, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d6 per Witch level

**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 6 + your Constitution modifier

**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d6 (or 4) + your Constitution modifier per Witch level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** None

**Weapons:** Simple weapons

**Tools:** One type of artisan's tool of your choice

**Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Charisma

**Skills:** Choose three from Animal Handling, Arcana, Deception, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Persuasion, Stealth

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a sickle or (b) a dagger
- (a) a component pouch or (b) an arcane focus
- (a) a scholar's pack or (b) an explorer's pack

### Spellcasting

You have studied to bend magic to your will, casting spells you have committed to memory.

#### Cantrips

You know three cantrips of your choice from the witch spell list. You learn additional witch cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Witch table.

#### Spell Slots

The Witch table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your witch spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these spells, you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest. For example, if you know the 1st-level spell Cure Wounds and have a 1st-level and a 2nd-level spell slot available, you can cast Cure Wounds using either slot.

#### Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher

You know four 1st-level spells of your choice from the [witch spell list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1y26juIH_8bWwAMkgOP1-0ypYEM73QJehyvFdrWdLjkY/edit?usp=sharing).
The Spells Known column of the Witch table shows when you learn more witch spells of your choice. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots, as shown on the table. For instance, when you reach 3rd level in this class, you can learn one new spell of 1st or 2nd level.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the witch spells you know and replace it with another spell from the witch spell list, which also must be of a level for which you have spell slots.


#### Spellcasting Ability

Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your witch spells. Your magic comes from your experiments and study of the ancient arcane. You use your Intelligence whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Intelligence modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a witch spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.
<div class="break"></div>

**Spell save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier
**Spell attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier



#### Ritual Casting

You can cast any Witch spell you know as a ritual if that spell has the ritual tag.

### Magick

Your experiments form themselves through Magick, additional effects your spells gain. Most Witches keep Notes of their experiments in books or scrolls, which often gets them mistaken for a wizard’s spellbook. However, only Witches can make use of another’s Magick Notes.
<div class="break"></div>

At 1st level, you learn two Magicks of your choice. When you cast a cantrip or spell, you may expend one of your Magicks and apply the effect based on its description. Only one Magick can be used per spell. You regain expended Magicks at the end of a long rest.
<div class="break"></div>

**Learning Magick.** When you gain a level in this class, you gain an additional Magick of your choice to add to your Notes. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.
<div class="break"></div>

**Preparing Magick.** You prepare the list of Magicks that are available for you to use. To do so, choose a number of Magick from your Notes equal to half your Witch level (minimum of two Magicks). You can change your list of prepared spells when you finish a long rest.
<div class="break"></div>

**Copying Magick Notes.** When you find Magick notes, you may copy it into your own Notes experiments. The process involves deciphering the unique methods of the author, practicing with the Magick, and making it your own.
<div class="break"></div>

The process takes 2 hours to complete plus an additional hour for each Magick you have already added through this process. If the Magick has a level requirement you do not meet, you can not prepare it until you do.


### Magick Refresh

At 2nd level, you can quickly restore a Magick at the cost of a spell slot. As a bonus action, you may expend a spell slot and regain an expended Magick.

### Witch's Coven

At 3rd level, you establish yourself as a practitioner of a coven, a group defined by their specialities and arcane craft. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Cantrip Versatility (Optional)
Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace one cantrip you learned from this class's Spellcasting feature with another cantrip from the cleric spell list.


### Efficient Experiments

Beginning at 7th level, you halve the casting times of spells that are longer than 1 minute. Also, the time required to write a new Magick into your Notes is halved.

### Damnation

At 13th level, your power overwhelms and cripples foes that do not withstand it. When a creature fails a saving throw against your spell save DC, it subtracts 1d8 to the next saving throw it makes until the end of your next turn.

### Witching Hour

At 20th level, become the nightmare they fear most. As a bonus action, spill forth mana from every pore and gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

- Once per turn when you use a Magick, immediately regain an expended Magick.
- When a creature is affected by the Damnation feature, decrease all saving throws it makes by 1d8 until the end of your next turn.
- You may use up to three Magicks when you cast a cantrip or spell. 

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Magicks

#### Curse of Chaos
One target of the spell is filled with Wild magic. At the start of its next turn, it rolls on the Wild Magic table.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** The amount of times it rolls on the Wild Magic table increases by 1 when you reach 5th level (2), 11th level (3), and 17th level (4). You choose which of the rolled outcomes the target takes.

#### Curse of Decay
One target of the spell is struck with sapping energy. For 1 minute, it has disadvantage on weapon damage rolls. It can end this effect by making a Constitution saving throw at the end of its turn.

#### Curse of Hellfire
One target of the spell is wreathed in hellish fire. For 1 minute at the start of its turn, it takes 1d4 + your Intelligence modifier fire damage. The target may make a Constitution saving throw at the end of its turn to quell the flames. The flames also extinguish if the target is fully submerged in water or takes cold damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** The Magick’s damage increases by 1d4 when you reach 5th level (2d4), 11th level (3d4) and 17th level (4d4).

#### Curse of Isolation
Prerequisite: Level 5
One target of the spell is mentally isolated from all others but you. For 1 minute, the target of the spell can only see you and all other creatures are considered Invisible to the target. The target may make a Wisdom saving throw at the end of its turn to end the effect. This Magick also ends if you are brought below 0 hit points or you are farther than 120 ft. from the target.
#### Curse of Seduction
One target of the spell has their mental defenses hindered. If the spell is a charm, the target has disadvantage on the saving throw.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** This Magick also overrides a target’s Immunity against the Charmed condition for the spell when you reach 11th level.

#### Doppler’s Blood
Immediately after casting the spell, you also cast the Disguise Self spell.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** This Magick instead casts the Seeming spell when you reach 11th level.

#### Falcon’s Eye
One target of the spell has their senses heightened. For 1 minute, the target adds a d4 on Investigation, Insight, and Perception checks.

#### Glamour
Wrap yourself in enchanting mana. For 1 minute, the target adds a d4 to all Charisma checks.

#### Glimmer
One target of the spell begins to glow with a bright light of up to 10 ft. and then a dim light of up to 20 ft.

#### Goliath’s Stone
One target of the spell is granted hardened skin. For 1 minute, they gain a +1 bonus to their AC.

#### Mistletoe
One target of the spell has their defenses stripped. Until the end of your next turn, they lose resistance to one of the following damage types of your choice: acid, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you reach 11th level, the damage type you choose can turn an immunity to a resistance.

#### Moon’s Howl
One target of the spell is imbued with the dark energies of the moon. For 1 minute, the target has resistance to non-silvered or non-magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage and deals additional damage on melee weapon attacks equal to its proficiency bonus. However, if the target starts its turn with less than half of its maximum Hit Points remaining, they must take the Attack action against the closest creature to them. If there are multiple creatures within the same distance, they choose the target randomly.

#### Resourcefulness
If the spell has a material component and you consume the exact materials needed without the use of an Arcane Focus or Component Pouch, the spell is upcast by 1 level. If the spell does not normally have a material component, you may cast it with one appropriate to its nature with approval by your DM. For example, casting Command and consuming a silver crown as the material component.

#### Respite
One target of the spell receives rejuvenating energy. For 1 minute at the start of its turn, it regains 1d4 Hit Points. The Magick ends if the target starts its turn with more than half of its maximum hit points or they are brought below 0 hit points.
<div class="break"></div
>
**At Higher Levels.** The Magick’s healing increases by 1d4 when you reach 5th level (2d4), 11th level (3d4) and 17th level (4d4).

#### Saltpetre
One target of the spell is protected from the unholy. For 1 minute, aberrations, fiends, and undead creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against them.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.**  When you reach 11th level, the target also has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, Frightened, or Possessed by those creature types.

#### Vampiric Fang
One target of the spell is leeched of their life force. The next time within 1 minute they take damage, you regain hit points equal to half of the damage taken.

#### Season of Change
*Prerequisite: Level 5*
Immediately after casting the spell or cantrip, you may exchange it for a different spell or cantrip in the Witch’s spell list. The new spell must be of the same level as the old one. The effects of this Magick are reverted upon taking a long rest.

#### Curse of Endings
*Prerequisite: Level 9*
One target of the spell that is concentrating on a spell is inflicted with divine energy of the End. The target immediately makes a Concentration check against your Spell Save DC.

#### Curse of Perpetuity
*Prerequisite: Level 9*
One target of the spell is instilled with divine energy of Perpetuity. For 1 minute, they have advantage on Concentration checks.

#### Curse of Self-Doubt
*Prerequisite: Level 5*
One target of the spell has its mind turned against it. For 1 minute at the start of its turn, it must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, they hesitate in their plans and may only take an action or bonus action on that turn. On a success, the effect ends.

#### Silver Steel
*Prerequisite: Level 5*
One target of the spell is blessed with sharp silver for 1 minute. The creature’s weapon attacks count as Silvered and a +1 magical weapon, provided it does not already have an equal or greater bonus.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** This Magick’s magical bonus increases by +1 when you reach 11th level (+2).

#### Safety
*Prerequisite: Level 9*
One target of the spell is surrounded by protective magic. The target has resistance to all damage types for 1 minute or until they take damage.

#### Trade Wounds
*Prerequisite: Level 9*
One target of the spell is linked to your life force and vice versa. For 1 hour, both of you have resistance to all damage. However, when one of you takes damage, the other takes the same amount of damage. This effect ends if one of you is ever farther than 60 ft. of the other or one of you is brought below 0 Hit Points.

#### Trial of Grasses
*Prerequisite: Level 9*
One target of the spell is inflicted with a painful process to open them up to change. For 1 minute, they have disadvantage on saving throws against transmutation spells or transformations.

#### Curse of Domination
*Prerequisite: Level 13*
One target of the spell has their mind twisted against them. Immediately after casting the spell, you also cast the Dominate Person spell on the target, which lasts for 1 round.
