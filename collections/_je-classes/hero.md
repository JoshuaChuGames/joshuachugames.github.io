## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 3

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 4
<div class="break"></div>
**Evasion:** 10

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 5 / 10
### Starting Equipment
A Hero starts their journey with the following items:
- One two-handed weapon or 2 one-handed weapons
- One set of armor
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points) or a Basic Potion of Stamina (regain 1d4 Stamina)
- An heirloom or object that reminds you of home

## Class Features
### Helping Hand
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

You are always nearby, willing to aid your allies. When an ally is in Very Close range and fails a **check**, you can spend 1 Stamina to add a d6 to their roll. Describe how you help them with their **check**.

### Close Call
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

Just when your enemies think they have you, you slip through their aggression. Once per session, when an enemy succeeds on an Attack **check** against you, spend 1 Miracle to avoid the attack.

# Subclasses
## Inspiring
A beacon of hope or triumph to those who see you, you excel at bringing out the best in others

**Primary Trait:** Charisma

### Inspiration
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

Once per session, spend 3 Stamina and describe how you rally your party to face the odds with renewed fervor. All of your allies who witness you gain a Miracle.

### Stay With Me
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Your presence gives your allies greater conviction to stay alive. When an ally within Close range would make a Death Action, spend 1 Miracle to let them regain 1 Hit Point.
<div class="break"></div>
## Paragon
An agile warrior feared by those who stand against your values, you strike faster than the eye can see.

**Primary Trait:** Agility

### Repetition
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you make an attack, you can spend 3 Stamina to make a number of additional attacks equal to your Hero Tier.

### Dash
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Your body is honed for agile movements. When you make an Agility **check** to move far distances, double your Agility modifier added to the check.
