## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 3

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 2

**Starting Mana (Tier 1):** 4

<div class="break"></div>

**Evasion:** 6

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 3 / 6
### Starting Equipment
A Mage starts their journey with the following items:
- One magic focus
- One one-handed weapon
- One set of armor
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points) or a Basic Potion of Mana (regain 1d4 Mana)
- Notes on a theoretical spell from your master, left for you to now complete.

## Class Features
### Visualization
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

In order to cast your spells, you must first visualize exactly what you wish to happen. When a spell has you make a **check**, you may spend 1 Stamina to gain advantage on the **check**.

### Focused Mind
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

You can hold onto concentration with intense focus. Once per session, when you would lose concentration on a spell, you may spend 1 Miracle to maintain concentration on the effect.

# Subclasses
## Studied
One who has dedicated many hours to learning and understanding magic, you have a vast amount of spells at your disposal.

**Primary Trait:** Knowledge

### Deep Learner
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you study spells to learn them, you pave the way for the next spell in your path. Whenever you spend **Skill Points** to learn a new spell, you gain **Spell Skill Points** equal to the spell’s Tier Requirement. You can spend **Spell Skill Points** to learn spells.

### I Know a Tome
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

With your time spent studying, you have gathered libraries' worth of niche knowledge. When you make a Knowledge check, recall lore about magic or the arcane, you may spend a Miracle to triple your Knowledge modifier added to the check.

<div class="break"></div>
## Instinctual
One who has focused entirely on the visualization of your magic, you have a talent for augmenting spells.

**Primary Trait:** Instinct

### Augmenting Adept
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

You have an easier time augmenting your spells. When you spend Mana to augment a spell, you may augment it an additional time.

### To The Limit
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

You can push your spells to the limit in a pinch. Once per session, you can spend a Miracle when casting a spell to augment it as many times as possible without spending additional Mana.
