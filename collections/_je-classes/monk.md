## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 4

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 5

<div class="break"></div>


**Evasion:** 9

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 4 / 8
### Starting Equipment
A Monk starts their journey with the following items:
- One two-handed weapon or 2 one-handed weapons
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points) or a Basic Potion of Stamina (regain 1d4 Stamina)
- A martial manual or a book of poetry

## Class Features
### Unarmored Defense
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

Your body and limbs are conditioned to withstand blows and magic. You gain a \+1/2/4 bonus to your damage thresholds while you are not wearing armor.

### Humble Living
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

You have learned to live on very little. Once per session, you may spend a Miracle to regain a number of **Hit Points** and **Stamina** equal to your Monk **Tier**.

# Subclasses
## Wise
You have learned more than a few hard lessons in your life, allowing you to guide others on their journey.

**Primary Trait:** Instinct

### Words of Wisdom
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you take a short or long rest, share a story related to one of your **Experiences** with your allies. Each character that hears the story can use that **Experience** as if it were their own.

### Exposing Strike
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

You know just how to open up your foes. When you succeed on an attack, you may spend a **Miracle** to **Expose** them. While **Exposed**, the target can not reduce damage thresholds.

<div class="break"></div>
## Martial
You have learned to wield discipline through physical force, using your own body to strike back at those who would do you harm.

**Primary Trait:** Dexterity

### Martial Prowess
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you make a damage roll with a weapon or your body, the minimum damage die you roll is a d8.

### Dazing Strike
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Your attacks are mighty, capable of winding any foe. When you succeed on an attack, you may spend a **Miracle** to **Daze** them and force them to lose 1 **Stamina**. While **Dazed,** the GM can not activate them until the GM spends a **Doom** to end the condition.
