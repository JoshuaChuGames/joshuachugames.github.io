## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 3

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 3

**Starting Mana (Tier 1):** 3

<div class="break"></div>


**Evasion:** 6

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 4 / 8
### Starting Equipment
A Priest starts their journey with the following items:
- One two-handed weapon or one one-handed weapon
- One set of armor
- Holy scripture
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points) or a Basic Potion of Mana (regain 1d4 Mana)
- A priest’s emblem or amulet, given to you by your church or mentor.

## Class Features
### Bless
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

As a priest, you can confer blessings on your allies. At the start of a session, you have a number of **Blessings** equal to **Primary Trait**. When an ally in the same scene makes a **check**, you can spend a **Blessing** to add your **Primary Trait** modifier to the roll.

### Bestow Miracle
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

Your **Blessings** can lead to amazing outcomes. If an ally has no **Miracles**, you may spend a **Blessing** to grant them one. This **Miracle** is lost if unspent by the end of the session.

# Subclasses
## Healer
Your talents have led you to excel at alleviating injuries and ailments from others, making sure everyone stays alive on their journey.

**Primary Trait:** Knowledge

### Healing Touch
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

You can heal with a touch. Spend a **Blessing** to allow an ally within **Melee** range to regain 1d4 Hit Points.

### Medic
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Once per session, when you take the **Tend to Wounds** rest action, you may heal yourself and all allies resting with you. Describe how you patch everyone up.

## Enchanter
Your talents focus on bolstering those around you, ensuring that they fight stronger than ever.

**Primary Trait:** Knowledge

### Invigorating Touch
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

You can invigorate with a touch. Spend a **Blessing** to allow an ally within **Melee** range to regain 1d4 Stamina or 1d4 Mana.

### Oomph
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

When you cast a spell that targets an ally, spend a **Miracle** to let them add your Priest Tier to their damage rolls.
