## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 4

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 4

<div class="break"></div>


**Evasion:** 11

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 4 / 8
### Starting Equipment
A Thief starts their journey with the following items:
- Two one-handed weapons
- One set of armor
- A set of lockpicks
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points), a Basic Potion of Stamina (regain 1d4 Stamina), or a Basic Poison (inflicts 1 Poisoned)
- A stolen item from your first score or job

## Class Features
### Sneak Attack
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

You excel at exploiting weaknesses in your enemies. When you succeed on an attack with advantage or while an ally is within **Melee** range of the target, add a number of d6s equal to your Thief Tier to the damage roll.

### Hidden in Plain Sight
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

If you succeed an **Attack Action** against an enemy while **Hidden**, you may spend a **Miracle** to avoid losing the condition.

# Subclasses
## Delver
Your time robbing homes or plundering dungeons has honed your senses and movement, helping you to slip past defenses and detect traps.

**Primary Trait:** Dexterity

### Careful Step
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

You’re accustomed to moving around unnoticed. While **Hidden**, you may make a Primary Trait **check** to remain **Hidden** if you or an enemy moves into a space where they would normally spot you.

### Danger Sense
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

No trap or ambush can escape your notice. You can spend a **Miracle** to detect the presence of traps or **Hidden** enemies in the area.

## Connected
Your time interacting with others has built a large web of contacts, allowing you to call on their aid when necessary.

**Primary Trait:** Charisma

### I Know Someone
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

When you arrive in a new town or environment, you have a connection to someone who lives here. Describe this person, give them a name, and how they could be of use. Then, choose one of the following:
- They’re a shrewd person, always asking for something in return.
- They owe me a favor, but they’re wanted here.
- They always scam me.
- We used to be great friends but things got messy between us.
- We used to be a couple.

### Thief’s Cache
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Once per session, you can spend a **Miracle** to find a nearby cache of supplies that you or your connections hid for emergencies. Choose a number of the following items equal to your Thief Tier to be in the cache:
- A Basic Potion of Healing
- A Basic Potion of Stamina
- A Basic Potion of Mana
- 10 silver
- 50 ft. of rope
- A disguise
- A set of tools
- A forged identity for yourself or a member of your party
- A map of the region or town

You may select the same item more than once, and the GM may allow any mundane item to be in the cache.
