## Class Traits
**Starting Hit Points (Tier 1):** 5

**Starting Stamina (Tier 1):** 4

<div class="break"></div>


**Evasion:** 7

**Damage Thresholds:** 0 / 7 / 14
### Starting Equipment
A Warrior starts their journey with the following items:
- One two-handed weapon or 2 one-handed weapons
- One shield
- One set of armor
- A torch
- 50 ft. of rope
- A Basic Potion of Healing (regain 1d4 Hit Points) or a Basic Potion of Stamina (regain 1d4 Stamina)
- A weapon part from your master.

## Class Features
### Grit
*Tier 1 Class Feature*

Part of your training is withstanding and dealing pain. At the start of a session, you have a number of **Grit** equal to your Primary Trait modifier. When you make a **check** using your Primary Trait, you may spend a **Grit** to add a d8 to the **check**.

### Opportunity
*Tier 2 Class Feature*

You can react to enemies that attempt to escape your grasp. If an enemy within Melee range moves away from you, spend a **Miracle** to inflict one of the following effects.
- They are **Restrained** by you and can not move.
- They are **Exposed** by you.
- You deal damage to them with one of your equipped weapons.
- You move along with them.

# Subclasses
## Tough
You are trained to resist all threats, physical or magical, so that you may take the pain that others can not.

**Primary Trait:** Strength

### Withstand
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

No pain is too great. When you take damage, spend a **Grit** to reduce it by one threshold.

### Retaliate
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Strike back at your enemy. Your **Opportunity** feature may now be triggered when an enemy within **Melee** range deals damage to you.

<div class="break"></div>
## Brutal
You are trained to inflict unbelievable damage upon your targets, slaying them before they can do the same to you.

**Primary Trait:** Strength

### Eviscerate
*Tier 1 Subclass Feature*

None can resist your offense. When you succeed an **Attack Action**, spend a **Grit** to roll an additional damage die.

### Onslaught
*Tier 3 Subclass Feature*

Strike while they are weak. Your **Opportunity** feature may now be triggered when you succeed an **Attack Action** against an enemy within **Melee** range.
