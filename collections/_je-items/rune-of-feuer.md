A Rune that can be applied to a weapon, granting it additional properties. Unless otherwise stated, a weapon may only have 1 Rune at once, and you must be wielding the weapon to utilize the Rune. 

**Feuer.** When you deal damage with the weapon, spend stamina to inflict 1d4 Aflame to a single target.
