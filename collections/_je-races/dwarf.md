***Dwarves are naturally strong and durable people, often possessing talents in physically demanding professions. Dwarven culture often pushes them to master a single skill like blacksmithing or combat. Though they live for centuries, many still seek companionship with humans and their own kind.***

<div class="break"></div>
Dwarven adults often grow to be between 3 and 4 feet tall and live for about 4 centuries.
<div class="break"></div>
**Durable.** You have a \+0/\+1/\+2 to your damage thresholds.

<div class="break"></div>
**Inventive.** When you invent your own Spell or Technique to learn, the learning cost of the ability is reduced by 1\.
