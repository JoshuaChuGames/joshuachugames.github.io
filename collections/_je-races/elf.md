***Elves are a rare, often isolated people who are capable of living for thousands of years. To them, months could feel like a mere day. Elves tend to have difficulty with boredom and feel little urgency outside of life-threatening events. Being living witnesses to history, they are often well learned and well travelled.***

<div class="break"></div>
Elven adults often grow to be between 4 and 5 feet tall, and live for so long that many could consider them ageless.
<div class="break"></div>
**Expertise.** One of your Experiences starts with a \+4 modifier instead of a \+2.

<div class="break"></div>
**Unphased.** When you make a Basic Trait **check** to avoid or remove a Condition, you may spend a Stamina to add 1d4 to the total.
