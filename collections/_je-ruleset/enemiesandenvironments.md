# Enemies and Environments {#enemies-and-environments}

This section presents rules and stat blocks for enemies and environments

# Enemies {#enemies}

Your players will come across those who would operate against their characters. While not all enemies will do that through direct combat, the following describes how to read an enemy’s statblock.

## Statblock Breakdown

A **statblock** is a visual/textual representation of an enemy’s statistics, abilities, and even personality.

### Name

Each statblock has a name, which could be a general name like “City Guard” or a specific character’s name like “Larseo of Khare”

### Tier

Each enemy is supposed to go up against a specific power level of players. A well-balanced encounter would be against enemies with a Tier equal to the average of Class Tiers amongst the players. While you can use statblocks from other Tiers, you may want to adjust the exact statistics.

### Basic Traits

Most enemies have established Basic Traits to represent their abilities.

### Variant: Basic Modifier

Some enemies, especially minions or less important creatures, may just have a Basic Modifier for them to add to any given check.

### Evasion

All enemies have an Evasion to denote how difficult it is to attack them.

### Damage Thresholds, Hit Points, Stamina, and Mana

All enemies have these traits to establish their limits and resources in combat. They function the same way as they do for players.

### Attack Modifier

Combat-based enemies have an attack modifier to add to attacks.

### Standard Attack

Combat-based enemies have a Standard Attack that contains its name, range, and the damage on a hit.

### Experience

Some enemies may have Experiences that make them more capable. You can add an enemy’s Experience to a **check** by spending **Doom**.

### Features

Features represent an enemy’s capabilities in conflict.

# Environments {#environments}

Environments have statblocks as a way to help you run certain encounters and areas. While optional, they can be very useful for planning and executing your ideas smoothly.

## Statblock Breakdown

A **statblock** is a visual/textual representation of an environment’s statistics, abilities, and even description.

### Name

Each environment has a unique name that acts as a very short descriptor.

### Tier

Like enemies, environments have a Tier to go up against a specific power level of player characters.

### Description

A evocative but short snippet of the environment

### Impulses

Short phrases that provides the themes and also potential problems that the players may be up against in the area.

### Difficulty Class

Instead of basic traits, environments have a general **DC** that should be used for most checks being made in the area. You can adjust the **DC** as necessary if the player is attempting to accomplish something particularly easy or difficult for the environment

### Potential Enemies

Many environments will have a list of enemies that you can use to establish conflict or consequences for the actions of the players.

### Features

Like enemies, environment features are abilities and possible complications for you to use during the scene.
