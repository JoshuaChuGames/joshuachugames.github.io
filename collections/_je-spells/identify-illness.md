Identify the illness of a creature within Melee range. Choose up to **1** of the following facts to discover about the illness: You learn how the illness spreads or manifests within creatures. You learn how long the illness has been affecting the creature. You learn the symptoms of the illness throughout its lifetime in a creature. You learn of a potential cure, if there is one.
