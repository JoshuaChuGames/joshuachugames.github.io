A spell meant to conjure a simple but wondrous effect as evidence of the Goddess’s magic. Choose **1** of the following effects: A ray of moonlight or sunlight, depending on the time of day, begins to shine down on a point within **Very Close** range. A creature or object within **Very Close** range floats for a few seconds. A **cup or flask** of water is purified of contaminants or poison. You may work with the GM to produce additional wondrous effects with this spell.
