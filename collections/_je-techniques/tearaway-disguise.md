Needing only a few minutes to prepare, you can don a disguise of a person you have met before or an appearance you choose. While disguised, you have a **\+2** bonus on checks made to avoid detection. Start a progress counter that increments whenever you take an Action. When the counter reaches your Primary Trait, your disguise drops. You can prepare prior to using this Technique, having multiple disguises prepared. However, you can only have a number of disguises equal to your Primary Trait.
