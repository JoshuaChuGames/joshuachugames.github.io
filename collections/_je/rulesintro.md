## Introduction
Like other TTRPGs such as D&D and Daggerheart, Journey’s End is a role-playing game about telling stories within fantastical worlds. Specifically, Journey’s End borrows the worldbuilding and magic system from the popular manga and anime 葬そう送そうのフリーレン, also known as Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End. While some elements have to be extrapolated and interpreted to place into a gaming space, the hope is to recreate the feeling of fantasy that the series invokes.

### Magic is the Realm of Imagination
There are many different worlds that exist within Journey’s End. You could play within the world from the series or in one of your own creation. However, the mechanics of Journey’s End abide by one cardinal rule: Magic is the Realm of Imagination. What that means is that characters within the world of Journey’s End should be free to adjust, change, and improve upon their skills and abilities. That is not to say that anything a character believes in can be accomplished, as there should always be an appropriate cost to power, but the system should be incredibly modular and actively invite player’s to invent mechanics of their own.
