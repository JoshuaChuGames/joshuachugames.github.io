Hellish flames erupt around you, scorching your flesh before surging toward a creature within range. You take 1d8 fire damage, then make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 3d10 fire damage. The damage you take cannot be reduced in any way.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage you take increases by 1d8, and the damage dealt to the target increases by 1d10, for each slot level above 1st.
