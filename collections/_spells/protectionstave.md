You cause a pillar covered in protective runes to erupt from an unoccupied space on the ground within range. When you cast this spell, designate any number of creatures you can see.
<div class="break"></div>
The stave has AC 11 and 35 hit points. Whenever a designated creature within 20 feet of the stave takes damage, the damage is reduced by up to 5 and the stave’s hit points are reduced by the same amount. A creature can benefit from this damage reduction only once per round.
<div class="break"></div>
If the stave is reduced to 0 hit points, it crumbles and the spell ends.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the stave’s hit points increase by 10, for each slot level above 2nd.
