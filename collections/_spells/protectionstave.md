You cause a pillar covered in protective runes to erupt from an unoccupied space on the ground within range. When you cast this spell, designate any number of creatures you can see. The stave has AC 11 and 35 hit points. Whenever a designated creature within 20 feet of the stave takes damage, the damage is reduced by 5; a creature can benefit from this damage reduction only once per round. If the stave is reduced to 0 hit points, it crumbles and the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the stave's hit points increase by 10 for each slot level above 2nd.
