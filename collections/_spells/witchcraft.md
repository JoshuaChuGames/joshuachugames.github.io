You subtly adjust the appearance of a willing creature you touch. Choose one of the following effects, which lasts for the duration:

- Change the color of the creature's eyes or hair.
- Conceal or fabricate scars or minor skin imperfections.
- Alter the creature's voice, changing its pitch or timbre, or making it resemble the voice of a creature you have heard speak for at least 1 minute.
- Apply a cosmetic effect that could be created using nonmagical makeup.

The changes created by this spell are purely cosmetic and cannot alter a creature's size, shape, or overall identity. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
