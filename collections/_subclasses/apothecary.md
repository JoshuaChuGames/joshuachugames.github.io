***The Coven of Apothecaries is often hidden within civilization instead of away from it. They create elixirs and balms to heal or enhance. However, they are by no means harmless, and can concoct brews capable of great harm.***

| Witch Level | Features |
|-------------|----------|
| 3rd | Alchemical Notes, Alchemy |
| 6th | Suffer |
| 10th | Beg |
| 14th | Break |

### Alchemical Notes
At 3rd level, you can add recipes for potions to your Notes. Learn two potions that meet the level requirement according to the table below. Adding recipes uses the same process as learning a new Magick.

| Rarity | Level Requirement | Cost |
|--------|------------------|------|
| Common | 3 | 5 gp |
| Uncommon | 5 | 15 gp |
| Rare | 9 | 75 gp |
| Very Rare | 13 | 200 gp |
| Legendary | 17 | 1,250 gp |

### Alchemy
At 3rd level, at the end of a long rest, you may expend spell slots to brew potions containing a Witch spell cast with that slot. A potion can be consumed as a bonus action or thrown at a creature within 30 ft. as a ranged spell attack. Self-targeted and single-target spells affect the creature drinking or struck by the potion; area spells are centered where the potion spills; and the creature that last held the potion maintains Concentration. You may expend one Magick while brewing to add its effect. Unconsumed potions become mundane when you take another long rest.

Instead of a spell, you may brew a potion from your Alchemical Notes by expending its material cost. You can make a number of potions equal to your Intelligence modifier.

### Suffer
At 6th level, as a bonus action, you may end one of your Curses and force its target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target's speed is halved and it has disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls, and Constitution saving throws until the end of its next turn.

### Beg
At 10th level, as an action, you may end one of your Curses to gain advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against its target for 1 hour.

### Break
At 14th level, when a creature fails against Suffer, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn.
