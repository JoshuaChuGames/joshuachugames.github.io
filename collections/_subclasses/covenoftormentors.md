***The Coven of Tormenters is one most feared and ill-spoken of by outsiders. They utilize curses and hexes on their enemies to torture and kill, but also to protect. For those who have seen the most pain will often retaliate with horrors of their own.***

| Witch Level | Features |
|-------------|----------|
| 3rd | Malevolence |
| 6th | Suffer |
| 10th | Beg |
| 14th | Break |

### Malevolence
At 3rd level, your arcane experiments have been fueled by anger and the need to cause pain, empowering your magic the more you harm. Whenever you deal damage to a creature with a weapon attack or spell, you deal additional damage equal to the amount of curses you have applied to creatures.

A Curse is any of your Magicks that have curse in their name, such as Curse of Self-Doubt, the Hex spell, a Hexblade's Curse, or the Bestow Curse spell. Other effects may also count at the discretion of the DM.

### Suffer
At 6th level, as a bonus action, you may end one of your Curses and force its target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target's speed is halved and it has disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls, and Constitution saving throws until the end of its next turn.

### Beg
At 10th level, as an action, you may end one of your Curses to gain advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against its target for 1 hour.

### Break
At 14th level, when a creature fails against Suffer, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn.
