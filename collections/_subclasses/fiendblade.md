***You or your ancestor before had an intense and dangerous encounter with a creature from the Hells Plane of Fire that left you cursed to wield its volatile flames. While most would be unable to control their powers, wreaking havoc wherever they go, you have managed to harness your fire by channeling it through the metal of weapons.***

| Sorcerer Level | Features |
|------------|--------|
| 1st        | Fiendish Trait, Fiendish Blood, Hell's Arsenal, Forged in Fire |
| 6th        | Extra Attack, Burning Blood |
| 14th       | Fiendish Charm |
| 18th       | Firestorm |

### Fiendish Trait
Because of your hellish heritage, you have inherited a strange quirk from the Hells. Choose one from the following table or roll a d10:

| d10 | Trait |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | You grow horns and a tail similar to a Tiefling. |
| 2 | Open flames burn hotter and brighter when you are within 10 ft. of them. |
| 3 | You have a fear of snow and rain. |
| 4 | Your pain tolerance for heat and burns is extremely high. |
| 5 | Minor devils like imps naturally respect and fear you. |
| 6 | The whites of your eyes become black and your iris becomes a warmer color. |
| 7 | Holy water stings like salt on a wound, though it does not deal damage to you. |
| 8 | Your skin is slightly red or purple. |
| 9 | You always feel an urge to light something on fire. |
| 10 | You naturally give off some heat, often raising the temperature of a room. |

### Fiendish Blood
You learn additional spells when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Fiend Spells table. Each of these spells counts as a sorcerer spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of sorcerer spells you know.

### Hell's Arsenal
When you adopt this tradition at 1st level, you gain proficiency with two types of melee weapons of your choice.

### Forged in Fire
As a result of your hellish blood, you can bond with a weapon by spilling your blood on a weapon that lacks the heavy property and meditating with it for 1 hour. While bonded, you can recall the weapon back into your hand as an action as long as it is within 1 mile of you. Furthermore, while the weapon is being carried or wielded, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1) as long as you are not wearing armor or donning a shield.
- You have resistance to fire damage.
- When you cast a spell that deals fire damage, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1).

### Extra Attack
Starting at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your cantrips in place of one of those attacks.

### Burning Blood
Also at 6th level, your blood broils with hellfire. When a creature takes fire damage from your spells, it is lit on fire for 1 minute, taking 1d6 fire damage at the end of its turn. The target can put itself out as an action, by falling prone, being submerged in water, or taking cold damage.

### Fiendish Charm
At 14th level, you gain the ability to weave words together like a dealing devil. You can spend 2 sorcery points to gain advantage on a Charisma check. You may use this after rolling, though before the result is known.

### Firestorm
Beginning at 18th level, you can stoke the fires in your blood to temporarily amplify your Fire magic. As an action, you can spend 5 sorcery points to start a Firestorm for 1 minute. While Firestorm is active, you may choose a creature within 30 ft. to set on fire at the start of your turn.
Also, the damage die of your Burning Blood increases to a d10.
