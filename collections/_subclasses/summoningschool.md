***Summoning is the art of conjuring creatures and objects to aid you in your endeavors. Wizards of the School of Summoning are often characterized by having many “helpers” around them at all times, even if they are not expecting danger. They are often joked about needing a summon to do everything for them, from eating to walking. However, most do not dare to say it to their faces, lest a flock of hawks descend upon them.***

| Wizard Level | Features |
|--------------|----------|
| 2nd          | Summoning Savant, Quick Command, Dedicated Summon |
| 6th          | Specialized Summon |
| 10th         | Sustained Summon |
| 14th         | Perfect Summon |

### Summoning Savant
Beginning when you select this school at 2nd level, the gold and time you must spend to copy a Conjuration spell that summons a creature with its own initiative is halved. You may add Conjuration spells into your spellbook as if they were wizard spells if they are not in the wizard spell list.

Creatures created by spells you cast or class features count as your Summons. You may use an action to command all your Summons, allowing them to take actions and bonus actions on their turns. Otherwise, your Summons can only move and take the Dodge or Dash action on their turn.

### Quick Command
Starting at 2nd level, you can exert command of one of your Summons as a bonus action, allowing it to take actions and bonus actions on its turn. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus and regain expended uses at the end of a long rest.

### Dedicated Summon
Starting at the 2nd level, when you choose this school, you may enchant a summon with a special bond to make them more effective. A summon is a creature conjured by a spell that has its own initiative. As an action, choose a creature you have summoned that you can see within 30 ft. While bonded, the following benefits are granted:
- If the spell used to summon the creature has a duration less than 24 hours, it remains for 24 hours or until it is otherwise dispelled.
- The summon is treated as a familiar as if it were summoned by the Find Familiar spell.
- The creature may take the Attack action, even if the spell does not allow it.
- When you take damage, you may use your reaction to transfer half of the damage taken to your summon while it is within 30 ft. of you.

Once bonded, you cannot use this feature again until the end of a long rest. You gain an extra use of Dedicated Summon at 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Specialized Summon
Beginning at 6th level, you have shaped your Dedicated Summon to fulfill your exact needs, which manifest as Specializations. You gain three Specializations of your choice, which use your spellcasting modifier. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class. You gain two more Specializations at 10th, 14th, and 18th level.

When you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the Specializations you have and replace it with another Specialization that you could learn at that level.

### Sustained Summon
Beginning at 10th level, when any of your Summons are reduced to 0 hit points, you may expend a spell slot as a reaction to cause it to regain 1d10 hit points per level of spell slot plus your wizard level hit points. A Summon can only be affected by this feature once per long rest.

### Perfect Summon
Beginning at 14th level, your Summons have reached a greater level of autonomy. You have unlimited uses of the Quick Command feature.

### Specializations

#### Brutal Summon
When your Dedicated Summon attacks a creature, it adds your Intelligence modifier to the damage roll.

#### Protection of the Weave
While your Dedicated Summon does not wear armor or hold a shield, it adds your Intelligence modifier to its AC.

#### Spell Summon
You may cast a spell through your Dedicated Summon as its action. The spell must have a casting time of 1 action and cannot be concentration. Once per long rest, you may cast a concentration spell through your Dedicated Summon, but it immediately loses concentration at the start of its next turn.

#### Pack Tactics
*Prerequisite: Dedicated Summon has a climb speed.*
Your Dedicated Summon has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target and the ally is not incapacitated.

#### Flyby
*Prerequisite: Dedicated Summon has a flying speed.*
Your Dedicated Summon does not provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

#### Water Breathing
*Prerequisite: Dedicated Summon has a swim speed.*
Your Dedicated Summon can breathe underwater.

#### Summon Sense
While your Dedicated Summon is within 100 feet of you, you can communicate with it telepathically. As an action, you can see through its eyes and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of its special senses. During this time, you are deaf and blind with regard to your own senses.

#### Warrior Summon
*Prerequisite: You cannot have Primal or Mount Summon.*
Your Dedicated Summon gains proficiency with martial weapons, medium armor, heavy armor, and shields. Its Strength or Dexterity score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

#### Mount Summon
*Prerequisite: You cannot have Warrior or Primal Summon.*
Your Dedicated Summon becomes a Large creature and can carry up to two Medium creatures at once. Its Strength score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

#### Primal Summon
*Prerequisite: You cannot have Warrior or Mount Summon.*
Your Dedicated Summon's Maul attack deals 2d8 damage and its speed increases by 10 ft. Its Strength score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

#### Mind Shield
Your Dedicated Summon uses your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throw modifiers for saving throws with those abilities, as long as they are higher.

#### Switcheroo
As a bonus action on your turn, you may switch places with your Dedicated Summon.

#### Attention Grabber
Your Dedicated Summon may use its action to force all hostile creatures within 20 ft. that can see it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, a creature must spend its next turn attempting to harm the Summon.

#### Tough Summon
Your Dedicated Summon gains two additional hit points per level you have in this class.

#### Resilient Summon
Your Dedicated Summon gains proficiency in two saving throws of your choice.

#### Trained Summon
Your Dedicated Summon increases two of its ability scores by 2, to a maximum of 20.

#### Skilled Summon
Your Dedicated Summon gains proficiency in three skills or tools of your choice.

#### Eloquent Summon
Your Dedicated Summon can speak any languages you know and gains proficiency in Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion checks. Its Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.

#### Lifesteal Summon
Once per round, when your Dedicated Summon deals damage to a creature, it gains temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
